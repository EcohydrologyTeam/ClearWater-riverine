ClearWater-Riverine Documentation
=================================

Welcome to ClearWater-Riverine's documentation!

The ClearWater-riverine package is a two-dimensional (2D) water quality transporter model to calculate conservative advection and diffusion of constituents from an unstructured grid of flows within complex river systems and floodplains. It is developed with modern Python by the U.S. Army Engineer Research and Development Center (ERDC), Environmental Laboratory (EL).

The goal of this model is to simulate the transport (advection and diffusion) of heat and water quality constituents in riverine systems by coupling it to ERDC's ClearWater (Corps Library for Environmental Analysis and Restoration of Watersheds) modules that simulates water quality processes and kinetics.
